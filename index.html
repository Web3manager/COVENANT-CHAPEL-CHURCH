Who We Are

Planted in Uyo,
Growing for Eternity

Covenant Chapel Church International Ministries is an apostolic ministry established in Uyo, Akwa Ibom, with a mandate to bring the transforming power of God's Word to every family, community, and nation.

Under the leadership of Amb. Apostle Alexandar Nwakwor Uduntuk, we believe every life carries a divine covenant that God is faithful to fulfil.

We are a full-gospel ministry that believes in signs, wonders, miracles, deliverance, and the practical Word of God for everyday living.

"The covenant of God over your life is greater than every obstacle the enemy has placed before you. You were not born to survive — you were born to prevail."

— Amb. Apostle Alexandar Nwakwor Uduntuk
